Dynamic Callout v3

Adaptive contextual card for the Trade tab's hover callout. The card answers why this candle matters, not what its values are. Design comp — measured, not shipping code. Every gloss is hinglish.ts's; every real number is already published; prices are synthetic.

light is the default — theme.ts storedMode()

The four rules the card obeys

RuleHow the card honours it
C1The headline is the gloss, which names the KIND only. Direction comes from tone (evDir), never inferred from the kind. No gloss states a number.
C4The engine's own sentence is quoted verbatim under ENGINE KE APNE SHABD. The card promotes it; it never rewords it.
C2call / risk / lean drive the meter and the priority order. A risk never borrows a read's wording.
C13The measured % is labelled signal quality, never expectancy, and is excluded entirely from the rotation card — those numbers are scored on the fixed 30-minute clock C13 refused.

The colour mitigation. A chip that mirrors an object also drawn on the chart keeps the chart's hue and is shown filled (WALL 24700). The new hues apply only to card-local chips with no chart counterpart. That is what stops a purple chip and a brass balloon describing the same object.

Unchanged from today's computePosition — right of cursor, vertically centred, flips left near the right edge, clamped 6px inside every edge. One change is required: callers pass fixed ASSUMED_W/H constants today, so with three card sizes the flip would misplace S and L. The resolver must pass each state's own measured size.
