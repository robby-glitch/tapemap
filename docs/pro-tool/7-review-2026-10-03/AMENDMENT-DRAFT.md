## 2. The rule (one)

**GAVE UP.** While she is **at entry** (her give-up G has not yet trailed past her entry), the word is FOLD when all three hold:

1. **Her defenders came off their peak.** That is rev 6's OI event on W, unchanged: a drop of 3σ√n or more from the since-mark peak, n ≥ 3 updates, ≥ 3 % of the peak. W is the wall rev 6 already names behind her.
2. **They left as price came at them.** At the event's own timestamp, the future was ≥ w against her entry E: d·(fut − E) ≤ −w. The future is still ≥ w against E when the word fires.
3. **The order flow is not with her.** Neither the FLOW row nor the VPIN row is WITH. If either is WITH, the rule waits. It fires on the first row where (1)–(3) all hold, while that event is still live.

Printed: **"FOLD · 22700 PE writers gave up 10:35 with price at them"**. One sound, once per epoch, as rev 6.

**Its complement is the pullback line.** It adds no word and no sound.

- While at entry, with the future ≥ w against E and W still building or stopped (not off), HOLD's clause leads with **"pullback · 22700 PE writers still there 119.4L"**. Her four reads follow it, as rev 6 prints them.
- It is condition-born: it appears after 2 (h) bucket closes and leaves after 2 (h).

**Line 2 at entry** becomes: "out if **22700 PE writers leave while price is against you**, or 22769 is accepted".

**Seller:** the same engine with d flipped, in the seller's own sentence: "FOLD · 22900 CE writers gave up 12:06 with price at them".

**SENSEX:** its FLOW and VPIN read "could not check", so condition 3 never blocks there. The rule is OI plus price, and it says so in the clause.

## 4. What it gives up

- **It only cuts losers.** It never fires while she is winning, so it does nothing for her early exits on winners. On 28 Sep she left at +30 % with all four reads WITH and her give-up already locked at about +11 %; the put ran to +97 % (REPLAY §2). The word was already right there; this rule cannot help her press it.
- **Writers who leave before the price comes are ignored.** On 24 Sep 09:57 and 10:30 the zone longs wait for G at -13 % and -11 %, where candidate (i) gave -8 % and -2 %.
- **Out before a bounce.** On 30 Sep it is out at -14 %. The call later printed +23 % at 12:48 before dying at -45 %. On 25 Sep, A2 and 22 Sep 12:03 it is out before late run-ups of +59 %, +78 % and +63 %. Rev 6 was out of all four too, at a lower premium.
- **It does not fix the whipsaw.** Rev 6's at-entry FOLD comes back inside G within 20 min on 12 of its 24 rows (REPLAY §3, D3). This rule adds folds; it removes none.
- **No cap on G.** A trade with distant writers and no event still carries a -30 % give-up (13 of 45 replays).

## 6. Predictions, falsifier, kill (forward rows only; counts, never rates)

- **Falsifier, per GAVE UP fold:** it is *noise* if, within 20 (h) min, W prints above the event's peak **or** the future trades ≥ w her side of E. Otherwise it is *real*. The outcome row is written once at +20 min (rev 6 §4 form).
- **Kill 1:** with ≥ 15 GAVE UP folds in force (shadow rows never count), the rule is killed if the noise verdicts outnumber the real ones. Rev 6's at-entry rule then stands alone. Under 15 is inconclusive and nothing changes.
- **Kill 2, her problem:** each GAVE UP fold is logged in one of two columns:
  - **shaken out:** the premium later printed above her entry premium before 15:00;
  - **saved:** the premium later printed ≥ 10 (h) points of entry below the fold's premium.
  With ≥ 15 folds, the rule is killed if shaken out outnumbers saved.
- **Defect, not result:** a GAVE UP whose OI peak was set before her mark (rev 6 kill 1).
- **The noise budget:** rev 6 kill 6 applies unchanged. A GAVE UP is a FOLD, so ≤ 1 sound an hour. The pullback line counts as a clause change, so ≤ 1 headline/clause change an hour.
- **Logged for her, counts only:** each pullback spell, and how it ended (price back, G accepted, writers gave up, 15:00). In replay, 54 spells of ≥ 3 min ended 30 / 11 / 11 / 2 (REPLAY §4.4).

## 7. The strongest argument against

- **It is fitted, thinly.**
  - Each of its two new conditions rests on one row: zone 30 Sep 10:27 for "born under pressure", zone 25 Sep 09:54 for the flow guard.
  - 4 of its 7 changed rows are the same 22 Sep expiry afternoon, and two of those act on one OI event. That leaves 4 sessions of evidence.
- **It acts on small dips.** 5 of its 7 events are drops of 3.1 % to 5.4 %, close to the dips rev 6's T4 review found withdrawn (12 of 21 events: 1.1 to 3.9 % on 3 or fewer updates).
- **The risk:** forward, it could become a nervous pre-trail exit on a 3 % OI wobble. The 30-session forward log, not this table, decides. Kill 1 and Kill 2 are written for exactly that.

## 8. Not part of the rule: a defect -- FIXED 2026-10-03 (fold._prem_at; test_fold.py pins it; design replay still 9/9 MATCH)

- **"At your give-up" (and "at the wall ahead") is priced in the wrong space.** `fold._prem_at` uses `kp = K + F* − px`, where px is a futures-chart price (G, or A as `ak + bas`). The correct value is `kp = K + F* + basis − px`, i.e. K + (fut − px).
- Measured against what the premium did when the future reached G:
  - **30 Sep:** median error 34.0 premium points shown against 2.5 corrected;
  - **1 Oct:** 29.3 against 2.8;
  - 22 Sep: 5.4 against 1.8;
  - 23 to 25 Sep: no difference, because the basis was about 0.
- On 30 Sep it showed **152 (-8 %)** at her give-up. The corrected value was **113 (-31 %)**. At the FOLD it showed 178 while the call traded 127.55.
- A one-line fix, display only: premium is never an input to the verdict. Now pinned by `test_premium_at_a_futures_price_is_priced_through_the_basis`.
