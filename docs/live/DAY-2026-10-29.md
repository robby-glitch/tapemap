## 4. Findings, all of them

### 4a. Gaps she found that we did not (full list + reasons: `docs/kahani/review/GAPS-AND-LIVE-CHECKLIST.md`)

1. **The fuel was option-side** (call writers covering + put writers building). Jarvis and my reads only had futures OI. (A161)

### 4b. What I found checking her ideas

5. **"Writers stopped" alone is a PAUSE, not a top**, on 2 days [M]:
   - 29 Oct: 09:44, 09:59 and 10:14 each came back within 5–10 min, and the move ran +96f after;
   - 28 Oct: 10:48 and 11:32 did the same.
   - **TOP / BOTTOM RISK** = the stop + 3-min delta ≥ 2× its day median against, within 9 min. That gave 29 Oct 10:38 (right) and 28 Oct 13:19 (late, right way). (A165)
6. **Put writers moving the floor UP is also fuel ▲**, not only call writers covering. It drove 12:54–13:30. (A167)
7. **The afternoon rally was futures SHORT COVERING:** fut OI fell −1,361 lots after 13:00 while price rose ~180f. Such rallies stop when the covering ends (the 14:27–15:11 box). (A171)
8. **Effort without result at the high:** 4 pushes stopped at 23,055–23,059 while writers on both sides covered. That is squaring into the close, not a new push. (A173)
9. **All 5 morning zone SELLs were against the trend**, and so was BANKNIFTY's 14:10 SELL. (A161, A171)
10. **READ's word lags the writers:** "TREND ▲ since 10:10" while the writers turned down at 12:42. (A166)

### 4c. Why the AI keeps missing these (her question; full text in the GAPS doc §2)

- one feature at a time from the last message, each with its own narrow inputs;
- an AI's default sense is a BUYER's (price and delta first), while in Indian index options the writers ARE the market;
- levels, not rates;
- one strike, not the band;
- rules designed off one day;
- no forced cross-check (my live read never called /api/board or /api/sides);
- silent holes;
- the writers' 2×2 not used as the state.

### 4d. The live checklist (now standing: memory `live-read-full-checklist`)

The read goes in this order:
1. **Health.**
2. **Location:** bands, σ, levels, zone.
3. **Flow + SPEED:** delta, CVD, effort, signs, delta ÷ median.
4. **WRITERS:**
   - the 2×2 per slot, writer flow ±7, walls and their migration;
   - the handshake, who is trapped;
   - PCR only as a change.
5. **Futures OI quadrant.**
6. **Cross-index.**
7. **Her setup / gear.**
8. **ONE call:** wrong-line, two scenarios, and both a buyer and a seller expression.
