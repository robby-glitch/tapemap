## D. HOLD v3 candidates (shadow lines; the pass bar written 23 Oct 22:30, before any row exists)

**The lines** are `holdshadow.py`, shown under HOLD v2 and logged to `data/hold/shadow_<day>.jsonl`. Each one is HOLD v2 plus one extra exit, paired on the same trade, with R on HOLD v2's own risk.

- **CHOP**: on a CHOP / PIN-typed day (§1o, the DECODE log at that minute), a fade (entered on the far side of the 5-min fut VWAP from her direction) takes its profit at VWAP. No RIDE convert (A38).
- **POP**: a trade bought into covering (a payers' front UNWINDING at entry) folds at the first 3-min close where:
  - the covering has stopped;
  - price is at a fresh extreme her way;
  - the tape is exhausted (EXHAUST / ABSORB / a long's effort ≥ 2×, within 15 min).
  - SENSEX: could not check.
- **EFFORT**:
  - a long folds at the first DECODE close with an expensive fresh high (≥ 2×);
  - a short holds past HOLD v2's exit while the latest fresh low is cheap (≤ 0.5×), and exits when it is not;
  - NIFTY [I tested], BANKNIFTY [I], SENSEX could not check.

**They are not rules.** A line becomes a rule (HOLD v3) only by passing this bar, and then only on her signature, as a new registration. HOLD v2 (§1m) stays frozen meanwhile.

**The bar, per line, on forward rows only (from her Monday 26 Oct restart; nothing before counts):**
- **Counted:** rows with `applies` true and `final` true, her marked or imported trades. `applies` false (not a fade, not bought into covering) and could-not-check rows are counted separately, never pooled.
- **n ≥ 15 paired trades over ≥ 8 sessions.** Under 15 is inconclusive (house rule 4).
- **Pass** needs all four:
  - the mean of dR (line − HOLD v2) ≥ +0.20R;
  - the median dR ≥ 0;
  - with the best trade dropped, the mean dR stays > 0;
  - no single day carries more than 40 % of the summed dR.
- **Judged once at n = 15.** If it passes, judged again at n = 30, and it must pass both times.
- **A line that fails** is deleted from the window. Its rows stay in the log.
- **No hit rate is printed, ever.** The judgement is a table of dR rows plus these four numbers.
