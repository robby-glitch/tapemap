## 1. What it is, in one paragraph

Mary's own diagnosis: *"identifying direction is never my problem but holding a trade is: closing too early …
getting panicked with even 1 single red candle, or holding a bad trade in FOMO"*. So she has two tools.
- **The HOLD / FOLD window** reads the exact trade she is in, every finished minute, as HOLD / WATCH / FOLD /
  PATA NAHI. It floats over every tab, can pop out over her broker, and exists only while she is in a trade.
- **The Validate tab** does two things:
  - before a trade, one VALIDATE press gives GO / WAIT / NO and four facts;
  - after "I'M IN", the same tab reads the trade the way the window does.

THE PLAN adds the stop, the next wall and the room. The MARKET NOW strip puts the Board's market reads on the
Validate tab. Advisory only: no order path, never a size, never odds.

## 3. Where the code is

**Pure rules:**
- `hold.py`: the window;
- `plan.py`: the plan;
- `validate.py`: the word.

**The logs:** `hold_log.py` writes write-once logs to `data/hold/`:
- `hold_<day>`: trade rows, `rung_touch`;
- `side_<day>`: side rows, `stamp_track`;
- `check_<day>`: VALIDATE presses, rev 1 and rev 2 rows, and `check_outcome`.

**Server:** `server.py`, the `_hold_*` functions and the `/api/hold*` routes.
- `GET /api/hold?id=&rows=brief`, `/api/hold/side`, `/api/hold/trades` (which carries `lots`), `/api/hold/check`
  (`&log=1` = a logged press).
- `POST /api/hold/trades` takes the ops mark / exit / reopen / unmark. A double-click is refused, and a
  cross-site POST gets a 403.
- `POST /api/hold/import` takes the tradebook CSV at the close; it adds `exit_t_broker` alongside her own
  exit time.
- Trades, late closes and B reads are kept in `data/day_state/`, so the day survives a restart (the anytime-start
  rule).

**UI:** `ui-v2/src/hold/`:
- `store.ts`: ONE poller for the tab and the window, polling every open trade;
- `parts.tsx`: the shared pieces;
- `HoldTab.tsx`, `HoldWindow.tsx`, `MarketNow.tsx`;
- `hold.css`, scoped under `.hv`, LIGHT only.

The shell's MACHINE strip, the tab title and the Glass board now read the **zone** machine (`zone_state`).

**Tests:** `test_hold.py`, `test_hold_route.py`, `test_plan.py`, `test_validate.py` (81). The full suite is 1539
collected, with 3 known failures in `test_desk_numbers.py`.

## 7. House rules for whoever continues

- **Registrations come before code.** Signed by her ("sign"), anchored with a hash in `research-findings.md`,
  adversarially reviewed first. Never tune on the data a rule was disclosed on.
- **Never a hit rate, a win rate, odds or a confidence score.** A count, never odds.
- **Three distinct absences:** "checked, none", "could not check" and "not shown". Every number carries [M] or [I].
- **LIGHT only.** No charts: she reads charts on her broker.
- **Never a size, never an order path.**
- **The agent never starts or restarts the live server** on 8765; she does. No restarts during market hours.
- **VALIDATE presses in a test browser must strip `&log=1`,** or they enter her forward record. Stub the POSTs.
- **`server.py` is CRLF.** Never commit `data/` (`trigger_log.jsonl` is irreplaceable). Commit only on her word.
