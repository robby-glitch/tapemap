## 1. Verdict in 5 lines

1. **Would a pro desk use this today?** As a pre-trade checklist, maybe. As the thing that tells her to hold or fold, **no**.
2. **Overall: 4.5 / 10.**
   - The discipline (pre-registration, three absences, [M]/[I], write-once logs, the candle-lag fix) is better than most prop-desk in-house tools.
   - The typography of the word is right.
3. **Day one of live use.**
   - The window folded her 12:01 NIFTY 23100 CE on a **1.4-point one-minute close, 60 seconds after entry**. That call finished +29 %.
   - The Validate word counts a stop **2–3 points away** as "Room to run — WITH YOU".
4. **The consequence:** the tool currently *manufactures* the "1 single red candle" exit it was built to cure. It shows its own FOLD's reasons wrongly (a for-fact printed as the against-reason). It lets a FOLD be ✕'d away, watches only one of two open trades, and hides its two most important qualifiers ("noise", "structure only").
5. **The one thing that most keeps it from 10:** the line's break rule. A 1-minute close with zero buffer, paired with a Validate rule that rewards tight stops, puts her into trades whose first wiggle is a FOLD. Every other fix is secondary: a trader who sees one wrong FOLD learns to ignore all of them.

## 3. BRUTAL FINDINGS

**Tags used below:**
- **[REG]** — needs a new registration: it changes a rule, not its presentation.
- **[PRES]** — presentation only.
- **[BUG]** — code wrong against its own spec.
- **[HER CALL]** — an interpretation question for Mary, not a rule change (the BUILD-REPORT "revision questions" class).

### P0: wrong or dangerous for a trader

**P0-1 · The line breaks on a one-point, one-minute close, so the window itself is the "1 red candle" panic. [REG]**
- **Evidence.** Her live trades, `/api/hold?id=…&rows=brief` at 15:32:
  - **NIFTY 23100 CE** long, filled 12:01:21 @ 113.55.
    - `plan.entry.stop.room = 2` pts [M] at the 12:00 read.
    - `fold = {t: 12:02, rule: 3}`: "NIFTY closed 23,073.8 below your line 23,075.2", which is 1.4 pts.
    - Premium at 15:29 was 146.95, +29 %.
  - **NIFTY 23200 PE:** FOLD 12:30 on "23,087.5 above your line 23,084.8", which is 2.7 pts.
  - The prereg itself logged the hazard: "buffer 0 produced a 2-point break on 09-22 11:14" (`PREREG-merged.md` §7).
  - Code: `hold.py:174`, `beyond = c > L if bear else c < L`.
- **At the desk.**
  - Scenario: she buys a CE, the index dips 1.4 pts through a 5-minute swing low, and 60 seconds later the header goes red.
  - This is her diagnosis verbatim ("panicked with even 1 single red candle"), now signed off by the tool.
  - The first FOLD she sees live is wrong. After that she ignores the one that is right, and she did: the PE's exit was marked about 50 minutes after its 12:30 FOLD (a click time, see P1-9).
- **Fix.**
  - **[REG]** A break the tape's own noise cannot trip. The candidates, no number invented:
    - a close beyond L on the **3-minute** bar (CLAUDE.md rule 3: "3-minute is canonical");
    - or two consecutive 1-minute closes;
    - or a buffer equal to the index's own median 1-minute range over the last 30 minutes [M].
  - Register it and re-run the 33-trade probe.
  - **[PRES], today, no rule change:**
    - print the break size in the FOLD sentence ("closed 1.4 pts through your line");
    - print the entry room in the entry check ("you entered 2 pts from your line").
  - A trader can see a marginal FOLD for what it is.

**P0-2 · "Room to run" counts a stop that is about to be hit as WITH YOU. [REG]**
- **Evidence.**
  - `live-validate-no.png`, 14:12 NIFTY 23100 PE: "Room to run — WITH YOU — next wall 19 pts away, your stop 3 pts away".
  - Her 12:00 CE entry: stop 2 pts, rung 23 pts. Under `validate.py:45-48` that is WITH YOU.
  - The rule compares two distances and has no floor.
- **At the desk.**
  - The tighter the stop, the likelier this fact votes *for* her. So the Validate word pushes her toward exactly the entries P0-1 folds in a minute.
  - The two rules compound: GO/WAIT invites the trade, and FOLD kills it on the first wiggle.
- **Fix.**
  - **[REG]** WITH only when the stop room exceeds the index's own recent 1-minute range [M] *and* the rung room ≥ the stop room. AGAINST when the stop sits inside one candle's noise.
  - **[PRES] today:** under the STOP block, print "your stop is inside one candle's range (median 1-min range N pts [M])" when it is. It is a measured fact, and no vote changes.

**P0-3 · "Your setup" calls her setup AGAINST while the MACHINE strip on the same screen shows it ARMED her way. [REG] + [PRES]**
- **Evidence.**
  - `live-validate-no.png`: MACHINE "ARMED … SELL · u3" (a put buyer's setup, CLAUDE.md rule 3). Fact 1 on the same screen: "the latest zone trigger is BUY d2 at 13:00 — the other direction" at 14:12. That is **72 minutes old**.
  - `validate.py:22-30`:
    - takes the day's latest *trigger* with no age limit;
    - ignores the *arm* (`server.py:2195-2197` computes both and passes both).
- **At the desk.** A stale opposite trigger outvotes a live setup in her direction. Two panels on one screen disagree about the one thing she built the machine for.
- **Fix.**
  - **[REG]** An age window for the trigger, and a registered role for a fresh arm in her direction.
  - **[PRES] today:** the reply already carries `ago`. Print "BUY d2 at 13:00, 72 min ago", and print the latest arm beside it.

**P0-4 · A FOR fact is printed as the reason for WATCH (and can be for FOLD). The forward log records the contradiction. [BUG] / [HER CALL]**
- **Evidence.**
  - `hold_trade_1000.json` runs, and `in.png`: "09:55 WATCH — PE 22,850 folded 09:52, 9% off its 09:39 high". For a long PE, a *PE* (payers') fold is good news.
  - Root cause, `hold.py:370-377`: `_payers` returns the `q_fold` "for" text **before** it checks `qA` (payers against, held 3 min).
    - `hold.py:300` then uses `P["fact"]` as the WATCH reason whenever `qA` is true.
    - `hold.py:281` and `:292` do the same in the rule-6 and rule-4 FOLD sentences.
  - The row shows ✓, the count counts it *for*, and the word says WATCH.
  - `_hold_row` logs `payers.state = "for"` on a WATCH minute (`server.py:2282`).
- **At the desk.** She reads "WATCH — the put writers are covering" and cannot tell what is wrong. The real reason, the nearer PE front building in her way for 3 minutes, is invisible.
- **Fix.**
  - In `_payers`, test `qA` before `q_fold`, and carry the fold as a clause: "PE 23,100 adding in your way (3 min) · PE 22,850 folded 09:52".
  - The word is unchanged; the row state and the count change. That is **her call**, as the BUILD-REPORT Q1–7 were.
  - Add a regression test on the 09:55 minute.

**P0-5 · The two qualifiers that carry the product's honesty and its anti-panic message are stripped from every headline. [PRES]**
- **Evidence.**
  - `parts.tsx:31`: `strip()` removes `^(HOLD|WATCH|FOLD|PATA NAHI)…? — `. As a result:
    - "HOLD · structure only — OI could not check: …" renders as a big **HOLD** and "OI could not check: …";
    - "HOLD · noise — what holds this trade has not changed …" renders as **HOLD** and "What holds this trade has not changed …".
  - `now.tag` is never rendered in:
    - the tab band (`HoldTab.tsx:386`);
    - the float head (`HoldWindow.tsx:240`);
    - the collapsed line (`HoldWindow.tsx:216`).
  - It survives only as grey 12 px text in the runs list.
  - §3.2 is explicit: "A plain HOLD is never shown with OI blind."
- **At the desk.**
  - **Structure only:** at 09:20, with the fronts forming, she sees a clean green HOLD built on price alone.
  - **Noise:** at 12:15 her premium is red. The one word written for that moment, NOISE, is not on the screen.
- **Fix.** Render `tag` in the word slot on all three surfaces:
  - "HOLD · NOISE" in the word colour;
  - "HOLD · PRICE ONLY" in `--unknown`;
  - "HOLD · NOTHING PAYING 7/15" in caution ink.

**P0-6 · ✕ can hide a FOLD for the rest of the trade. The BUILD-REPORT claim "closing can never hide the FOLD" is false. [PRES]**
- **Evidence.**
  - `HoldWindow.tsx:124` re-opens only on the *transition* into FOLD (`was && was !== 'FOLD'`).
  - Rule 1 latches FOLD, so the transition happens once.
  - ✕ pressed during FOLD (`:207`) hides it until she marks another trade.
  - `lastWord` is a ref. If the page reloads and FOLD fires on the first poll, `was` is undefined: no tone and no re-open.
    - Reloads are not rare: the UI was edited live twice today (14:20 and 14:45, BUILD-REPORT), and a dev-server full reload or an F5 does the same.
- **At the desk.** This is the FOMO path exactly. She is in the trade, FOLD goes red, she does not want to hear it, she presses ✕, and the tool goes silent while she holds.
- **Fix.**
  - On FOLD, **✕ becomes "I'M OUT"**. The only way to dismiss a FOLD is to say you are out. That is not a tone and not an alert, so it is inside §5.
  - Store `{id, word}` at close, and re-open from server state (`rd.fold != null && closedWord !== 'FOLD'`), not from a client-side transition.

**P0-7 · With two open trades, only one is watched; the other can FOLD silently. It happened today. [PRES]**
- **Evidence.**
  - `/api/hold/trades`: NIFTY 23100 CE (12:01:21) and NIFTY 23200 PE (12:05:28) were both open until 13:23:18/20.
  - The CE was FOLD from 12:02.
  - `HoldWindow.tsx:101-103` shows `current` only.
    - The tick (`:117-128`) polls, tones and re-opens for `sel` only.
    - The "Open trades" list (`:299-306`) shows contract and time, **no word**.
  - `HoldTab.imIn` selects the *last* marked trade. From 12:05 the window followed the PE, and the CE's FOLD had no presence at all.
- **At the desk.** Any second position (a hedge, a flip she forgot to close, a straddle) becomes invisible to the tool that is supposed to watch it.
- **Fix.**
  - Poll every open trade: one batched GET, or N GETs.
  - The header carries the **worst** word across open trades (FOLD > PATA NAHI > WATCH > HOLD).
  - Every row in the list shows its word and since-time.
  - Tone on any trade's transition into FOLD.

**P0-8 · The buyer's quiet clock is hidden on the tab and absent from the collapsed line. It is her own FOLD rule, registered "visible at all times". [PRES]**
- **Evidence.**
  - §3.5: "The clock is visible at all times ('paid last HH:MM · quiet n of 15'), so this FOLD never surprises."
  - The tab prints it only inside the collapsed `<details>` ("Deeper detail"), via `Rows` (`parts.tsx:130`; `HoldTab.tsx:425`).
  - The float prints it at 12 px under the rows (`HoldWindow.tsx:285`).
  - The collapsed line has none (`:213-232`).
- **At the desk.** At "quiet 13 of 15" she is two readable minutes from a rule-5 FOLD, and nothing she can see says so. Then FOLD "surprises" her, which is the exact outcome the registration promised away.
- **Fix.**
  - Put the clock beside the word on every surface for a buyer: "quiet 13/15", caution ink from 10.
  - On the collapsed line it replaces the rung text.

**P0-9 · After a FOLD, the STOP shows a different line from the one that broke, so a FOLDed trade can show "31 pts room". [PRES]**
- **Evidence.**
  - `hold.py:168-173` keeps trailing L after the break.
  - For her CE:
    - the FOLD sentence says line 23,075.2;
    - `now.line` says `L 23109.05, room 31, src "5m swing low 14:25"`.
  - For the PE:
    - the FOLD sentence says 23,084.8;
    - the collapsed bar says "line 23,055.5 · 69 pts past it" (`live-float-col.png`);
    - the STOP block says 23,055.5 (`live-in-fold.png`).
  - The current `StopBlock` adds "the line broke at 12:02" beside a level that did not exist at 12:02.
- **At the desk.** A FOMO holder reads a FOLD with "31 pts" of healthy-looking room as permission to stay.
- **Fix.**
  - Once `fold` is set, the STOP block and the collapsed line show **the line that broke** (keep L in the latch, as `latch.why` already does), labelled "broke 12:02 by 1.4 pts".
  - The trailed line goes to the detail as "since trailed to 23,109.1 — a new trade would use it".
  - Hide NEXT WALL after a FOLD ("the plan ended at the FOLD"). The live PE still printed "PE 23,100 GIVING WAY — in your favour" at 15:29.

### P1: wrong often enough to cost money or trust

**P1-1 · The big number is the wrong number on NO. [PRES]**
- **Evidence.** `HoldTab.tsx:278` prints `n_for`. The 14:12 band reads "NO · **1** of 5 line up", but NO fired because **3 were against** (`validate.py:66`).
- **At the desk.** "1 of 5" next to NO reads as "one thing is off", not "three are against you".
- **Fix.**
  - Show the count as five marks, ✓ ✕ – in fact order, beside the word: "✓ ✕ ✕ – ✕". That is still the count, and it cannot be read as 20 %.
  - Keep "1 with · 3 against" in words under it.

**P1-2 · One wall is two votes, and they can contradict. [REG]**
- **Evidence.**
  - Facts 4 and 5 are the same rung (`validate.py:41-54`).
    - 09:35: PE 23,100 DEFENDED 4 pts gives **2 against** from one strike.
    - 09:43: PE 23,000 GIVING WAY gives "Next wall WITH YOU" and "Room to run AGAINST" on the same wall.
  - A wall that is giving way does not cap the room. PLAN §2.7 itself says "the move may run past".
  - Trend is counted twice as well: in fact 2 (the HOLD reason) and as fact 3.
- **At the desk.** "4 of 5 line up" overstates independent agreement, and a single wall swings the word.
- **Fix.** [REG]:
  - one wall, one fact: room counts only while the wall is DEFENDED or STALLED;
  - trend counted once.

**P1-3 · The Validate word spends 3 of 5 votes on direction, which she says is never her problem. [REG]**
- **Evidence.**
  - Facts 1, 3 and 5 are direction reads.
  - Her words: "identifying direction is never my problem but holding a trade is."
- **At the desk.** The pre-trade question that would change her outcome is "will I be able to hold this?" That means:
  - stop room vs noise (P0-2);
  - whether anything is paying right now (the clock state at the check minute);
  - the time of day on 0–4 DTE (the expiry line is already in the reply);
  - the premium against its own VWAP (σ, already computed).
- **Fix.** Rev 2 of VALIDATE-PREREG with holdability facts. The five facts stay as a printed checklist.

**P1-4 · GO is possible while "Right now" is WATCH. [REG]**
- **Evidence.** `validate.py:68`: GO needs 0 against and ≥ 4 for, and WATCH is only NEITHER.
- **At the desk.** She presses GO, enters, and the window's first word is WATCH. That is the panic primer.
- **Fix.** [REG] GO requires fact 2 = HOLD.

**P1-5 · A writer can never get GO, and the screen says the room "could not be read". [REG] + [PRES]**
- **Evidence.**
  - `plan.py:138-140` gives a writer no rung.
  - `validate.py:43-44` then prints "the room could not be read" (a false *could-not-check*).
  - `:54` prints "no wall read".
  - So n_for ≤ 3 and GO is unreachable, silently. The SELL toggle is first-class on the slip.
- **Fix.**
  - [REG] writer facts (VALIDATE-PREREG never mentions writers).
  - [PRES] today:
    - render facts 4–5 for SELL as "not shown — a writer has no next wall; time is her target";
    - print once: "rev 1 cannot say GO for a writer: at most 3 facts can line up".

**P1-6 · The five facts collapse the three absences into one grey NEITHER. [PRES]**
- **Evidence.** All render as `— NEITHER` (`HoldTab.tsx:293-303`):
  - "no zone trigger on this index today" (checked, none);
  - "the room could not be read" (could not check);
  - the writer's rung (not shown);
  - a real STALLED wall (a value).
- **Fix.** The server tags each fact with `absence: none | blind | hidden | null`, and the UI renders the `Abs` style in the text cell.

**P1-7 · Changing lots forces a new, logged VALIDATE and disables I'M IN, at the worst moment. [PRES]**
- **Evidence.**
  - `HoldTab.tsx:56`: `key` includes `lots`; `:72` marks the answer stale; `:179` disables I'M IN.
  - `:160`: "+" stays disabled until the first answer ("the lot size comes with the first answer").
- **At the desk.** She validates 1 lot, gets GO, buys 2 lots at the broker, comes back and bumps lots to 2.
  - I'M IN greys out.
  - She must re-VALIDATE a minute later, and may now get WAIT.
  - The trade is then marked against an answer she did not act on.
- **Fix.**
  - Drop `lots` from the stale key.
  - Scale the ₹ risk client-side (`risk / qty × newQty`).
  - Fetch the lot size at mount: `_hold_lot` exists, so add it to a GET.

**P1-8 · I'M IN can mark the same trade twice; I'M OUT is one untyped click with no undo. [BUG]**
- **Evidence.**
  - `HoldTab.tsx:104-113`:
    - no busy state, no try/catch;
    - `t0: fill.t || nowHMS()` is evaluated per click;
    - the id carries the second (`server.py:2075`), so two clicks across a second boundary give two open trades.
  - I'M OUT (`HoldTab.tsx:114-118`; float `HoldWindow.tsx:304,309`, 28 px buttons in a list) needs no confirmation and has no undo.
- **Fix.**
  - Disable on click.
  - Fix `t0` when the answer lands.
  - Have the server refuse a mark within 60 s of an identical open contract.
  - Put an "Undo" on exit for 10 s.

**P1-9 · The exit time in her forward record is when she clicked, not when she exited, and nothing ever corrects it. [PRES] / [HER CALL]**
- **Evidence.**
  - `imOut` sends `exit_t: nowHMS()`, and there is no field.
  - `_hold_import` (`server.py:2450-2452`) imports unmarked trips but never reconciles a marked trade's exit with the tradebook.
  - Both of today's exits were stamped 13:23:18 and 13:23:20.
  - §7's clauses read `after_exit`.
- **Fix.**
  - An exit-time field prefilled "now", as on entry.
  - At the close, the import sets `exit_t_broker` beside the clicked one. It is additive and never rewrites a row; her call on which one §7 reads.

**P1-10 · The answer never ages, and FOLD reaches her one to two minutes after its close. [PRES]**
- **Evidence.**
  - "read the 14:12 minute, pressed 14:13:43" (`live-validate-no.png`).

  - There is no staleness-over-time on the tab (stale is only a contract change). The collapsed line carries no read time.

- **Fix.**

**P1-11 · The pop-out does not fit her screen and cannot collapse. It is still unverified in her Chrome. [PRES]**

**P1-12 · In the main window the "collapsed" line is a 960-px banner parked over the index tickers, and the open float covers the Board's right third. [PRES]**

**P1-13 · The tone is off by default and buried, and each tone leaks an AudioContext. [PRES]**

**P1-14 · σ is fetched from REST every minute per trade and never shown. It is a registered footer item, missing and undeclared. [PRES] + perf**
- **Evidence.**

## 5. What a top trading-tool designer would do differently (inside her rules)

- **Make a FOLD cost an action.**
  - Prop-desk risk monitors do not let a limit breach be closed. It is *acknowledged*, by someone, with a reason.
  - Here: on FOLD the window offers **I'M OUT** or **"I'm staying"** with one tap of why: "my line" · "my target" · "hope".
  - "Hope" is the FOMO confession. Logged beside the trade (an additive log; confirm with her), it becomes the evidence §8 already asks for: "she can act against the window, and that disagreement is the evidence".
- **A commitment device at entry.**
  - Before I'M IN, one tap: "I exit on FOLD" / "on the line" / "my own call".
  - On FOLD the window quotes her back to herself: "You said you exit on FOLD. FOLD since 12:30 (7 min)."
  - No order, no size. This is the evidence-based anti-FOMO mechanic, and it costs one row.
- **A post-close exit review, in counts only.**
  - Built from the forward log: "Today: exited during HOLD ×1 · stayed through FOLD ×2 (about 50 min and 81 min)."
  - Described rows, never a rate. That is Bloomberg's trade blotter post-mortem, aimed at her two named failures.
- **A monitor, not a panel.**
  - Bloomberg Launchpad's small monitors, Quantower's position panel and TradingView's order-panel strip carry one line of state per position and nothing else.
  - The pop-out should be that. Per open trade: WORD · since · room pts · quiet n/15 · age.
  - Everything else is one click away in the tab.
- **A keyboard-first ticket, like Kite and Quantower.**
  - C/P, B/S, ↑/↓ strike, Enter to validate, I for in, O for out.
  - The first second at a desk is spent on the hands, not the eyes.
- **A diff when the word changes.**
  - When HOLD turns WATCH, show the row that flipped: "PAYERS ✓→✕ 12:26 — PE 23,100 adding".
  - This is how pro risk screens explain a status change, instead of re-printing a paragraph.
- **The word in the title and the favicon.**
  - `document.title = "FOLD · 23200 PE · 7m"` and a red dot.
  - The browser tab strip and the OS taskbar then carry the state even when TapeMap is behind the broker window. That is display, not an alert.
- **Data-age honesty, as Bloomberg's delayed flags do.**
  - Every surface says how old its minute is.
  - Past 90 s the chip turns caution ink. Past `STALE_MIN` the word greys to PATA NAHI styling with the reason.
- **Answer-first numbers for a buyer, in points.** Sensibull's "at target / at stop" pair, done in index points: "stop 18 pts · wall 49 pts · quiet 3/15" right under the word. The five facts become the *why*, not the answer.

## 6. Arguments against her rules (separate; nothing above depends on these)

- **"No charts" costs her the fastest read of room.**
  - A one-dimensional ruler, `stop ├──●────┤ wall`, is not a price drawing: no time axis, no candles, no history. It reads in a quarter of the time "18 pts · 49 pts" takes under stress.
  - A word ribbon (a bar coloured HOLD / WATCH / FOLD over the minutes since entry) would have shown "FOLD for 50 minutes" in one glance at 13:20.
  - Both are charts of *the tool's state*, not of price.
- **"[M]/[I] on every number" costs ink exactly where the eye must be fastest.**
  - A per-block convention keeps the honesty at half the density: "all numbers [M] unless marked [I]" in the block label.
  - The literal rule adds a tag to every hero number.
- **"DRAFT until judged," printed on every word, trains banner blindness.**
  - Her own falsifiers need ≥ 10 sessions, so DRAFT will sit on the screen for weeks.
  - Show it once per surface, with progress: "DRAFT · session 1 of ≥ 10".
- **Pre-registration has no defect fast-path.**
  - A rule error proven by a single live example needs an amendment path that does not reset the unaffected clauses' forward clocks. Examples:
    - a 1.4-point break 60 s after entry;
    - a 2-point stop scored "room to run".
  - As written, every fix like P0-1 costs a new registration and a fresh 10–30 sessions. The cost of the rule is that she trades on a known-bad rule while it is judged.
- **"Never a percentage" needs a sharper edge.**
  - "(24% off)" is measured OI, not odds. Banning it would cost clarity for nothing.
  - Meanwhile "4 of 5" is read as 80 % by every human brain, whatever the caption says: the rule is broken in spirit by the fraction it allows.
  - Better rule: no *odds* (probability, hit rate, confidence); measured percentages allowed with [M]; the count shown as marks, not as a fraction.
- **Light only:** no argument. It is right for a daytime desk beside a busy chart.
