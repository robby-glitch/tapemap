# BUG FIXES 2026-09-28: five reads that measured wrong (no method change)

**Approved by the operator, 2026-09-28 about 10:30 IST.** Her words: *"go do the bug fixes"*.
- **The audit that found them:** `docs/review/TRADER-AUDIT-2026-09-28.md` (§3 bugs 1, 4, 5, 6, 9 and 13).
- **What happened:** her 09:35 FOLD on a winning NIFTY 23000 PE.
- **When it is live:** built during market hours, live only after her restart after the close.

**No rule, threshold, state, fold rule or log field moves.** Rev 4, rev 5 (with COPY-1), VALIDATE rev 2, PLAN rev 3,
EXPRESSION rev e and grounds stay as signed. Each fix makes a read measure or say what its signed text already claims.

The method changes the audit proposes (her four reads, no latch, no candle close) are **not** here. They are a separate
amendment for her signature.

| # | fix | where | before | after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The CVD rank no longer ranks a change against itself** (audit bug 1) | `grounds._rank`, `grounds.facts`; `board.aggression`, `board.headline`, the Board's CVD family; `hold.flow`; `server._hold_rec` | The current 15-min change sat inside its own comparison list: 1.0 at the day's first read, at least 0.5 (STRONG) at the second. Any CVD read "pressing" 09:35–09:44. | It ranks against today's **earlier** 15-min changes only. With fewer than 3 of them (`MIN_PRIOR`), the rank is **"could not check — n earlier 15-min changes today; sizing this one needs 3"**, never "small" and never "strong". The Board headline says "Pressure could not check …" instead of "Nobody pressing". The futures-OI quadrant reads "could not check" on the same count. |
| 2 | **Ladder bars count at their true close** (bug 9) | `server._board_facts`, `server._hold_rec`, the landing check in `server`'s hold minutes, `board.tape_events` | A 5-min bar starting at m counted only from finished minute m + 5, a minute after it closed. | It counts from m + 4, the end of its last minute. That is the rule `board._closed` already used. |
| 3 | **"Writers" is tagged as inferred** (bug 4) | `hold.py`: the eight PAYERS / HOLDERS sentences | "Put writers at 22,900 adding … (+62.1L) [M]" | "… (+62.1L) [M] (writers: [I])". The OI change is measured; that writers made it is inferred, since OI up can be new buyers. |
| 4 | **A front off its high says so** (bug 5) | `board.paying` | Printed "still adding" or "adding again, still 13.5L under its 09:33 peak" while 22,900 PE fell every minute from 09:33. | "up on the 15-min slope (+51.2L) but 4.4L under its 09:33 peak" once the front is at least `STALL_FRAC` (5%) under its high. BUILDING (the 15-mark slope) itself is unchanged. |
| 5 | **"paid at t" names what paid** (bug 6) | `expression.py` SPEED, `validate._paying` | "paid at 09:38: flow with you" when the pay was the payers' fold and flow read against. | It names the payers row if it is "for", else the payers' live fold, else "flow with you" only if flow reads with. |
| 6 | **An expired future is never picked** (bug 13) | `upstox_instruments._futs` (in `fut_key`, `lot_tick`, `resolve`) | `futs[0]` by expiry with no date filter. On a roll morning a cached dump handed the ladder, VPIN and book the dead contract. | The same `expiry >= now` filter the options already use. Checked on today's dump: before the Sep future's expiry (2026-09-29 23:59:59) it picks `NSE_FO\|68407` (SEP); after it, `NSE_FO\|48704` (OCT). |

**Consequences a reader must know:**
- **Fix 1 makes order flow blind from 09:35 until about 09:49.** That is 7 closed 5-min bars, which give 3 earlier
  changes. Before the fix, those fifteen minutes read strong by construction.
  - Blind order flow cannot make a rule-6 FOLD or a WATCH.
  - Grounds' calls read CVD blind in that stretch.
  - It cannot size a writer's squeeze either. The writer's own-side `dpx` is unchanged, because the rank is not used
    there.
- **Rows logged before the restart were read with the old code, and stay as written** (write-once). Today's
  `data/hold/*_2026-09-28.jsonl` rows are the record of the defect.
