## 3. What the brainstorm has settled (her words + my trader view)

1. **Why we loop:** senses before brain, screens before the trader's day, ~10 headline verdicts, no "done" test, the lab
   mixed into the cockpit. Fix = a playbook first, one screen that IS the playbook, everything else under LAB.
2. **Approach A (cockpit by phase) — she said "almost right".** Then she corrected me, and she is right:
   - Waiting until 09:45 misses the **opening drive** → the cockpit runs from 09:15 (opening drive, test→reverse, gap fill).
   - It must not be one-dimensional: she **buys or sells, CE or PE**, and some days pay **both sides**
     (5 Oct: CE morning, PE 10:20–12:10, CE 12:40–14:30).
3. **The core question the tool answers:** "what is this market paying for right now — DIRECTION, TIME or VOLATILITY —
   and who collects it, the buyer or the seller?"
4. **The playbook matrix (state → best way):** OPENING DRIVE · OPEN TEST→REVERSE · GAP FILL · TREND · RANGE/TWO-WAY ·
   PIN (expiry) · BREAKOUT/EXPANSION · REVERSAL · LATE/THETA. Each row: how the tool recognises it (existing pieces),
   best for a buyer, best for a seller, don't. Summary of the rows:
   - OPENING DRIVE: gap outside yesterday's range/value, first 5–10 min one-way, new OI one side → buy ATM after the
     first pullback holds (small); sell the run-over side at its wall; fold back through the open; never fade it.
   - TEST→REVERSE: first push rejects at PDH/PDL/value edge → trade back inside to VWAP / prior POC.
   - GAP FILL: gap fails back into yesterday's range → toward PDC / POC.
   - TREND: one-sided writers, 0 VWAP crosses, 1σ–2σ lane → buy ATM/ITM on pullbacks into the lane; sell at the
     writers' strike; never fade the bands.
   - RANGE / TWO-WAY: both sides writing, VWAP crosses → buy CE at the lower band, PE at the upper (her zone machine +
     fade gate), or sell CE at the top wall + PE at the bottom wall; never hold buys for a run.
   - PIN (expiry): between the two biggest-OI strikes → sell straddle/strangle; theta after 13:00.
   - BREAKOUT: both sides covering, opening range breaks, IV/VPIN up → buy the break; don't sell into it.
   - REVERSAL: winning writers cover, other side builds, VWAP reclaimed with flow → take the new side, fold the old.
   - LATE / THETA: after 14:30 and expiry → sellers' time; her 15:00 rule.
5. **Five gauges** the answer rests on, each = state word + one number + arrow; the answer counts agreement; a
   disagreeing gauge goes amber:
   PRICE (VWAP ±σ lane, crosses, value/PDH/PDL, opening range) · OI (writers at 9 strikes: build/stop/quit, walls) ·
   FLOW (CVD, last-bar delta at the level, PEHAL/DABAAV/KHULA/BADA PRINT) · FUT OI × price (new shorts / long unwind /
   short cover) · VOL (IV, buyer's tax/drag, VPIN, gamma, DTE).
   Principle: **OI + structure = what the day is; order flow = the exact moment; volatility = buy or sell the option.**
6. **The answer block, always the same order:** STATE → BEST (buy AND sell, ranked) → WHERE → FOLD → DON'T.
   In a trade the same gauges flip to HOLD / FOLD. Her fold rule: zone + flow / OI / VPIN, **never a candle close**.
7. **Four corrections I made to approach A:** phases are STATES not clock slots; all three indices always visible
   (pick the cleanest, outliers are information); the lines (spot | fut) never leave the screen; ≤ 6 checks, each must
   earn its place (unproven = grey "shadow").
