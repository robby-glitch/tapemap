## 1. Overall rating: **5 / 10**

The rev 6 hold face — the DESK, its floating window and the GoCharting panel — is a real prop-desk screen. It has one
word at 56 px. It has four rows in her order (ZONE · FLOW · OI · VPIN), each with a chip, the engine's own sentence and
an [M]/[I] tag. Her give-up and the wall ahead are numbers. CAN'T CHECK always comes with its reason. That part earns
an 8.

Everything around it pulls the score down:

- **It is buried.** The DESK is the 12th of 14 tabs, labelled "Validate". The app opens on **Heat**, a pulsing spike radar.
- **Contradicting engines.** Eleven tabs carry the legacy engine's BULLISH / GO / MAX PAIN band, which the research
  scored below do-nothing.
- **Six answers to "is there a trade?"** They come from four different engines.
- **About a dozen state vocabularies.** One word, **fold**, means "writers unwound" on the Board and "get out" on the
  DESK.
- **Freshness signals that lie.** The green **LIVE** dot never turns off. "updated HH:MM:SS" ticks while every fetch
  fails. One slow poll tells her to run `stop.bat`. On a weekend the Board stamps Thursday's data with Saturday's date.
- **No shared colour grammar.** Red means CE on one screen, "against you" on another, "down" on a third. HOLD is green
  in the float's title bar and black in its body.

The core is lean and right. The shell and the legacy tabs are what make it hard to trade from.

### 2. Freshness that cannot lie
**What is wrong:**
- **The LIVE dot.** `App.tsx:397-400` paints a pulsing green **● LIVE** unconditionally. It sat next to "reconnecting…"
  and the red NOT LIVE banner all afternoon.
- **The timestamp.** `data.ts:1320` calls `setLastUpdated(new Date())` on every poll, even when all three indices
  failed. "updated 17:50:20" kept ticking while nothing was live.
- **The restart instruction.** One failed poll sets `'unreachable'` (`data.ts:1262, 1326`), and the banner then says
  *"Run stop.bat, then the TapeMap v2 shortcut again"* (`App.tsx:1721-1723`). I watched this fire twice in five minutes
  while the server was up and busy.
- **The TOKEN button.** When `/api/health` is slow, `broker` goes null and the **⟳ TOKEN** (Dhan) button appears inside
  the red banner (`App.tsx:1734`). Observed on the VPIN tab.

**Cost:**
- Mid-session, a 5-second stall tells her to restart the server, against her no-market-hours-restarts rule.
- A dot that is always green teaches her to ignore the dot.

**Smallest fix:**
- Render the LIVE dot only when `!error`.
- Set `lastUpdated` only on a successful tick, and print its age.
- Require 3 consecutive failures (~15 s) before `'unreachable'`.
- Between 09:15 and 15:30, drop the stop.bat sentence: say "backend slow — last good HH:MM:SS".
- Gate TokenCapture on `broker === 'dhan'`, never on `!== 'upstox'`. The Dhan fallback is **her keep-order**; the
  button stays, it just appears only where it can work.
