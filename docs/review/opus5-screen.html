The Rail — a docked column, not a dashboard. Meant to sit down one side of the screen beside your Kite chart, which stays the chart. Dark because your chart is white; no red and no green anywhere, because those are spoken for there. Amber is the side that is acting or forced. Violet is the side defending. Everything else is grey.

Three absences, three words, never merged. NOTHING FOUND = checked, negative · CAN'T CHECK = an input is missing, and which · NOT SHOWN = we have it and will not print it, and why. A hole is a dashed box the size of the value it replaces. Never a zero, never a bare dash, never a confident default.
