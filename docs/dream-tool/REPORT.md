## Review fixes (27 Oct, after the coordinator's six defects)

1. **Basis.** The per-minute raw basis (fut 1-min close − the chain's spot at that minute) is gone. `dream_facts.py` now
   records every chain snapshot's raw basis as fut LTP (the 1-s book) − spot at the same second (`basis_snap`), and
   `dream_build.py`'s `basis_at` is the median of those samples over the 3 minutes ending with the minute — fold.py's
   `BIX_MS` (3 × 60000, A60). Measured after the fix: 1-min steps over 8 pts fell from 72 / 78 / 50 / 60 / 68 per day to
   0 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 2, and every remaining step sits at 15:15–15:30 (the cash-auction freeze, a market fact). Every s|f pair,
   every converted level and the basis cell use it; DESIGN §1.6 says so.
2. **One level, one native number.** Levels carry `px0` (native) and `fr`; only the other frame converts, live. The plan,
   the chart tags, the rail and AAGE print "PDL s 22,848 → 22,868f" / "PDL f 22,866 → 22,846s"; a kind seen in both frames
   within 5 pts after conversion collapses to one row "PDL 22,848s|22,866f" (at fire in the brain, and again live on the
   face when the basis brings them together, e.g. 21 Oct 14:25 T1·T2).
3. **Clipping.** Rail widened (122 px) and every label fits; tile texts shortened (3 lines, no clamp); the TAPE's jump row
   and header are `flex:none` (no collision); the legend wraps on two lines; the header note pills moved into the NOW
   card. AAGE kind column 66 px; "+n" with the full list on hover.
4. **Rail piles.** Off-scale levels collapse to one edge marker per side ("▲ 6 upar" / "▼ 2 neeche", list on hover); an
   in-range label may slide one step (11 px); anything still colliding joins a "+n" chip with the list on hover.
5. **AAGE numbers.** Distances are signed with the unit ("+41 pts" / "−55 pts"); nodes within 8 pts merge ("ROKA +3");

  weekly options (13 Oct) imply +4 over spot while the 27 Oct future sits +55 — ordinary 19-day carry.
