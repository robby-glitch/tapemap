Each block is 5 minutes at one strike: the top half is the call side, the bottom half the put side. Order flow (Dext footprint) decides who it was: OI up with sellers hitting = writers; OI up with buyers lifting = option buyers (grey). Without a Dext chart for that strike the block is drawn from OI alone, faded. OI from TapeMap's Upstox chain; hits are the single biggest price cell of the 5 minutes at or above the index's floor (NIFTY 5L qty, SENSEX 0.2L: SENSEX ATM options trade about 1/65 of NIFTY's volume), with the size written from NIFTY 10L / SENSEX 0.5L.
